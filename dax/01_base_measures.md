## 1. Core Financial Measures

### Interest Income
Calculates the total interest income generated by the bank.

```dax
Interest Income =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[InterestIncome])
```

### Interest Expense
Calculates the total interest expense incurred by the bank.

```dax
Interest Expense =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[InterestExpense])
```

### Fee Income
Calculates the total income generated from banking fees and service charges.

```dax
Fee Income =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[FeeIncome])
```

### Operating Expense
Calculates the total operating expenses incurred by the bank.

```dax
Operating Expense =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[OperatingExpense])
```

### Provision Expense
Calculates the total provision expense recorded by the bank.

```dax
Provision Expense =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[ProvisionExpense])
```

### Tax Expense
Calculates the total tax expense incurred by the bank.

```dax
Tax Expense =
SUM('Fact_Banking_Monthly'[TaxExpense])
```

---

## 2. Derived Financial Measures

### Net Interest Income (NII)
Calculates the difference between interest income and interest expense.

```dax
NII =
[Interest Income] - [Interest Expense]
```

### Operating Income
Calculates operating income by combining net interest income and fee income.

```dax
Operating Income =
[NII] + [Fee Income]
```

### Net Profit
Calculates the bank's net profit after deducting operating expenses, provisions, and taxes from operating income.

```dax
Net Profit =
[NII]
    + [Fee Income]
    - [Operating Expense]
    - [Provision Expense]
    - [Tax Expense]
```
